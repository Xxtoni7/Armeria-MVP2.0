# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React for the frontend, Node.js with Express for the backend, and SQL Server as the database.

## Users

The primary user is a middle-aged person with basic knowledge of the products. They visit the website to explore the catalog, purchase legally enabled firearms, equipment, or accessories, and learn about document-management services. They need clear, personalized guidance throughout the purchase and related procedures.

## Product Purpose

Provide a simple ecommerce and landing experience where users can discover and purchase permitted products, understand the available services, and receive guidance for the corresponding legal and documentation procedures. Success means that users can find suitable products, understand the next steps, and access personalized support without unnecessary complexity.

## Positioning

The product brings together quality firearms-related products, personalized attention, and agile support for legal procedures in one place.

## Operating Context

Users browse a product catalog, review information about firearms, accessories, and equipment, and complete an ecommerce purchase flow. The site also explains the processes involved in firearm carrying permits and related procedures, and lists the legal-advisory and document-management services offered. It must work on desktop computers and mobile devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ecommerce catalog and purchasing for firearms, accessories, and equipment that are legally enabled for sale.
- Landing-page content explaining the business, processes, and available services.
- Legal-advisory and document-management service information, including firearm carrying permits and related procedures.
- Simple, clear, and accessible navigation across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- The applicable legal jurisdiction, exact eligibility and verification requirements, and the detailed purchase, payment, inventory, shipping, and authentication flows are open decisions and must be defined before implementation of those areas.

## Evidence on Hand

No additional product documentation, catalog data, testimonials, legal materials, or brand assets were provided during init. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make product discovery and purchasing straightforward.
- Explain legal and documentation procedures in clear language.
- Offer personalized guidance when users need help.
- Keep product and service interactions aligned with applicable legal requirements.
- Preserve a usable experience across desktop and mobile devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must maintain simple, clear, and accessible navigation across different screen sizes. A specific accessibility standard or compliance target has not yet been established.
